Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Draft for the Seshhon beta. It describes how the app works today and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Add the operator's name and a privacy contact before public launch (marked [ ] below).

Who we are

Seshhon is operated by [operator name and ABN]. In this policy, "we" means the operator. Contact us about privacy at [privacy contact email].

Who can use Seshhon

Seshhon is only for people aged 18 and over. At sign-up we ask for your date of birth to check your age. We do not keep your date of birth. We keep only a record that you were confirmed as 18 or over, and when. If you are under 18, please do not use Seshhon.

What we collect

  • Your first name, as you type it.
  • An account ID. There is no email address, phone number or password. The ID is stored in this browser, which is why clearing your browsing data signs you out for good.
  • Your invite code and your friend connections and requests.
  • Your status (On, Thinking or Off) and when it expires.
  • Seshes you start or join and the venue votes you cast.
  • Venue ratings and tags you give.
  • Deal codes you request and whether they were used.
  • Technical data such as your IP address and device details, which our hosting provider records in its logs for security and to keep the service running.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details.

Why we use it

  • To run Seshhon: show friends your status, run seshes and votes, show deals and confirm deal codes.
  • To keep it safe: prevent misuse, enforce the 18+ rule and investigate problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Friends see your first name, and your status only while you are On or Thinking, and only if they are On or Thinking too. When you are Off, you appear as Off.
  • Friends who are On or Thinking can see seshes you start or join, who has joined, and the votes.
  • Venue staff who confirm a deal code can see that the code is valid and which deal it is for.
  • Venue ratings are shown as totals and averages. Other people do not see which stars you gave.

Where it is stored and who handles it

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in [data region, e.g. Sydney, Australia — confirm]. The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those providers can see your IP address when you open the app. Some of these providers are based overseas, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they handle information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

How long we keep it

  • You can delete your account at any time in You → Your account → Delete my account. This removes your name, friends, votes and ratings.
  • Statuses expire automatically (On after 4 hours, Thinking after 2 hours).
  • Deal codes last 15 minutes. A record that a deal was used is kept for the night so a deal cannot be used twice.
  • Provider logs are kept for the period set by that provider.

Security

Data is sent over an encrypted connection. Access rules in the database mean you can only read what you are meant to see. We never put secret keys in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use Seshhon with that in mind. If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting [privacy contact email]. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
