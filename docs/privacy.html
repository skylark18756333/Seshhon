Privacy Policy

Draft for the Frendzy beta. It describes how the app works today and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Add the operator's name and a privacy contact before public launch (marked [ ] below).

Who we are

Frendzy is operated by [operator name and ABN]. In this policy, "we" means the operator. Contact us about privacy at [privacy contact email].

Who can use Frendzy

Frendzy is only for people aged 18 and over. At sign-up we ask for your date of birth to check your age. We do not keep your date of birth. We keep only a record that you were confirmed as 18 or over, and when. If you are under 18, please do not use Frendzy.

The age check

To make sure everyone on Frendzy is 18 or over, you may be asked to do a quick age check with our age check provider, [Yoti or Didit — confirm]. The check happens on the provider's own page, not inside Frendzy:

  • You take a live selfie and the provider estimates your age from it. If the estimate is under 25, you are asked to show photo ID (which is matched to your selfie) or use a digital ID instead.
  • The provider tells Frendzy only whether you passed, when, and which way (selfie, ID or digital ID). Frendzy never receives or keeps your selfie, your ID, your date of birth from your ID, or your estimated age.
  • The provider uses your selfie and ID only for the age check and deletes them once it is done, under its contract with us. Its privacy notice is shown before you start.
  • The provider may process the check outside Australia ([provider's processing region — confirm]). We take reasonable steps to make sure it handles your information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.
  • If the selfie shows you are clearly over 18, you do not have to show any ID.

What we collect

  • Your first name, as you type it.
  • An account ID. The ID is stored in this browser, so clearing your browsing data signs you out.
  • A username and password, if you choose to add them, so you can log in on another phone. We store your password and your recovery code only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read them. Wrong recovery code attempts are recorded against the username for one day to stop guessing.
  • An email address, when you add a username and password. We use it only to send you a 6-digit code to confirm it and each time you log in on a new phone, and a copy of your recovery code. It is never shown to other people, and we never use it for marketing. The codes are sent through our email delivery provider, which receives your address and the code. We keep only scrambled (hashed) codes, and a count of codes sent for one day to stop misuse.
  • Your invite code and your friend connections and requests.
  • Your status (Green, Amber or Red) and when it expires.
  • Seshes you start or join and the venue votes you cast.
  • Messages you send in a sesh chat, and the people you block or reports you make.
  • Venue ratings and tags you give.
  • If deals are switched on: deal codes you request and whether they were used.
  • Technical data such as your IP address and device details, which our hosting provider records in its logs for security and to keep the service running.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment details. If you tap Near me on the venue map, your phone works out which venues are close by on the phone itself; your location is not sent to us, stored or shown to anyone, and it is forgotten when you close the app. The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which sees which part of the map your phone is loading, as with any website. If Google ratings are switched on, our server asks Google for a venue's rating using only the venue's name and location; nothing about you is sent to Google. The age check selfie and ID go to the age check provider, not to us (see above).

Why we use it

  • To run Frendzy: show friends your status, run seshes and votes, show deals and confirm deal codes.
  • To keep it safe: prevent misuse, enforce the 18+ rule and investigate problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Friends see your first name, and your status only while you are Green or Amber, and only if they are Green or Amber too. When you are Red, you appear as Red.
  • Friends who are Green or Amber can see seshes you start or join, who has joined, and the votes.
  • Messages in a sesh chat are visible only to the people in that sesh, except anyone you or they have blocked. People in the sesh can screenshot or copy messages, so only send what you are happy for them to keep.
  • Venue staff who confirm a deal code can see that the code is valid and which deal it is for.
  • Venue ratings are shown as totals and averages. Other people do not see which stars you gave.

Where it is stored and who handles it

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in [data region, e.g. Sydney, Australia — confirm]. The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub can see your IP address when you open the app. The app's fonts are kept with the app itself, so no font service sees you. When you sign up, Cloudflare Turnstile may run a quick check that you are a person, not a bot, so Cloudflare also sees your IP address and browser details at that moment. Some of these providers are based overseas, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they handle information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

How long we keep it

  • You can delete your account at any time in You → Your account → Delete my account. This removes your name, friends, votes and ratings.
  • Statuses expire automatically (Green after 4 hours, Amber after 2 hours).
  • Seshes, who joined them and the venue votes are deleted as soon as the sesh ends, and in any case no later than 8 hours after it started.
  • If you start signing up but never finish, the unfinished sign-in is deleted after 2 days.
  • Sesh chat messages are erased when the sesh ends, and in any case no later than 8 hours after the sesh started.
  • If you report a message, we keep a copy of it, with the report, for up to 90 days so we can look into it. Only the operator can see reports.
  • If deals are switched on, deal codes last 15 minutes. A record that a deal was used is kept for the night so a deal cannot be used twice, then deleted the next day.
  • Provider logs are kept for the period set by that provider.

Security

Data is sent over an encrypted connection. Access rules in the database mean you can only read what you are meant to see. We never put secret keys in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use Frendzy with that in mind. If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting [privacy contact email]. If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
